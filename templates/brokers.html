Robinhood publishes no brokerage API of its own — its only official programmatic surface is crypto. The supported way to read a Robinhood account is SnapTrade, an aggregator that holds the broker relationships. You sign in on Robinhood's own page; this app never sees a Robinhood password.

To switch it on, create an app at dashboard.snaptrade.com — the free tier covers five connected accounts — and set SNAPTRADE_CLIENT_ID and SNAPTRADE_CONSUMER_KEY in the environment.

Robinhood through SnapTrade is read-only: positions, balances and order history. Placing trades is not available for Robinhood on any third-party integration.
