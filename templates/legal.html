Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Tradestaar Elite ("Tradestaar", "we") is a market research, charting and trade-planning app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party ads.

What we collect

  • Account details: your username and a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • What you create in the app: watchlists, chart drawings and layouts, price alerts, journal entries, notes, trade plans, research records, paper-trading orders and your app settings.
  • Broker connections (optional): if you connect Charles Schwab or a broker through SnapTrade, we store the access tokens those services issue (encrypted) and read balances, positions and orders to show them to you. If you turn on live trading, orders you preview and confirm are sent to your broker and logged in your account.
  • Notifications (optional): if you allow notifications, we store your device's push subscription; if you set up text alerts, the phone number you give us.
  • Basic technical data: server logs (time, page requested, IP address, browser type) used to keep the service secure and working. Kept for a limited time.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, health data or browsing activity outside the app, and we don't use tracking or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To run the app for you: show your lists, charts, alerts, research and broker data, and send the notifications you asked for.
  • To keep accounts secure (sign-in protection, preventing abuse).
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the features you use, and only what each needs:

  • Market data providers (for example Charles Schwab, Alpaca, Finnhub, Yahoo Finance, SEC EDGAR) receive the ticker symbols being looked up — not your identity.
  • Your broker (Charles Schwab, SnapTrade) when you connect it, under their own privacy policies.
  • AI providers (for example Anthropic or Nebius) receive market and research text to write summaries; we don't send your account details.
  • Messaging providers (web push services, Twilio for text alerts, Telegram if you set it up) to deliver alerts you turned on.
  • Hosting (Render) stores the app and its database.

We may disclose information if required by law. We don't sell or rent personal information.

Keeping it safe

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, broker access tokens are encrypted in our database, and repeated failed sign-ins are locked out. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your choices and deleting your account

  • You can disconnect a broker, turn off notifications, and delete watchlists, alerts and entries at any time.
  • You can delete your account in the app: Account → Delete account. This removes your account and the data stored for it from our database right away. Backups roll off within 30 days.
  • You can also email {{ support_email }} to request access to or deletion of your data.

Children

Tradestaar is not intended for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Common questions

  • How do I delete my account? Account → Delete account. It removes your account and its data.
