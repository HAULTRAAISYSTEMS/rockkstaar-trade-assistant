        Data coverage, sources and methodology

            Historical returns use end-of-session ledger valuations. Cash inflows are assumed to arrive at the opening and outflows at the close. XIRR uses recorded cash-flow dates and is withheld when the sequence can admit multiple solutions. Performance begins at the first supported closing valuation.

            The S&P 500 Total Return Index includes reinvested dividends. Comparisons use matching endpoints. Sharpe and Sortino use a 0% risk-free rate. Volatility is annualized with 252 sessions. Stock contribution excludes unallocated cash interest, taxes and account fees.

            To reconcile an internal transfer, add the same transfer:reference note to its two ledger entries. Record both entries on the same effective date. For unrelated cash movements that happen to match, add the note external-transfer. Tax figures use ledger FIFO unless the ledger has other verified basis adjustments.
